About RialtoBot

If you're reading this because you saw RialtoBot in your server logs — welcome, and thanks for checking. Here's what it is and how to stop it if you'd rather it left you alone.

What is RialtoBot?

RialtoBot is a small, polite web crawler that visits the public websites of local restaurants, cafes, and other small businesses to read what's already there — your name, hours, address, phone number, and menu (items, sections, prices). We use this to build a starter page for your business, so that if you ever want to claim it, most of the data-entry work is already done.

We are not a search engine, an ad network, or a data broker. We read the same public pages a customer's browser would load, once in a while, and nothing more.

What we collect

Only what's already publicly visible on your own website:

  • Business name, address, phone number, hours
  • Menu items, sections, and prices
  • Basic facts like whether you take reservations, offer delivery, etc., if your site says so

We do not collect anything from third-party sites (Google, Yelp, delivery apps, social media) — only from pages on your own domain. We don't create accounts, log in anywhere, or access anything that isn't already visible to any visitor.

How RialtoBot behaves

  • Identifies itself honestly. Every request carries this exact User-Agent string, with a real, working link back to this page — never anything else, never disguised as a browser.
  • Respects robots.txt. If you disallow us, we stop.
  • Rate-limited. We wait several seconds between requests to your site and never fetch more than a handful of pages per visit.
  • No evasion, ever. We don't solve CAPTCHAs, route around blocks, use proxies to hide our identity, or access anything behind a login.
  • We stop the moment you ask. See below.

RialtoBot/1.0 (+https://bot.rialto.st/; contact@rialto.st)

Opt out / takedown

If you'd rather we didn't visit your site at all, or want anything we've already collected removed, email contact@rialto.st with your business name and website. We'll confirm and honor the request within 24 hours — no forms, no runaround.
