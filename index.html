About RialtoBot

You found RialtoBot in your server logs. Here is what it is, and how to make it stop.

What is RialtoBot?

RialtoBot is a small crawler.

It is not a search engine. It is not an ad network. It is not a data broker. It reads the same pages a customer's browser would load, once in a while, and nothing more.

What we collect

Only what your own website already shows:

  • Business name, address, phone number, hours
  • Menu items, sections, and prices
  • Simple facts like reservations or delivery, if your site says so

We do not touch third-party sites. No Google, no Yelp, no delivery apps, no social media. Only pages on your own domain. We do not create accounts, log in, or see anything a visitor could not already see.

How RialtoBot behaves

  • Honest. Every request carries this exact User-Agent string, with a real, working link back to this page. Never disguised as a browser.
  • Respects robots.txt. Disallow us, and we stop.
  • Slow. We wait several seconds between requests and never fetch more than a handful of pages in one visit.
  • No tricks. We do not solve CAPTCHAs, dodge blocks, hide behind proxies, or go around a login.
  • Stops the moment you ask. See below.
